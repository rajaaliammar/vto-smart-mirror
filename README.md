PROJECT PROPOSAL: AI-POWERED VIRTUAL TRY-ON
(VTO) SYSTEM
Next-Generation 3D Fitting & Interactive Smart Mirror Solution for Fashion Retailers
Target Market: Fashion & Apparel Stores Core Technology: Computer Vision & Deep
Learning 
Deployment: In-Store Kiosk & Mobile Application
1. Executive Summary
The global retail industry is rapidly shifting towards interactive customer experiences. One of the biggest challenges
customers face in physical clothing stores is the inconvenience of trying on multiple garments in traditional fitting
rooms. Conversely, retailers struggle with high fitting-room queues, misplaced inventory, and lost sales opportunities.
This proposal outlines the development of an **AI-Powered Virtual Try-On (VTO) Smart Mirror & Software System**.
Using advanced Computer Vision (CV), real-time body tracking, and deep learning image warping algorithms, the
system enables shoppers to digitally preview how any outfit—from brand-new arrivals to past catalog archives—
looks and fits on their body in real-time, without physically changing clothes.
Key Value Proposition for Stores
Increases customer engagement, speeds up the decision-making process, unlocks access to 100% of the store's
inventory (including out-of-stock or past collections), and provides retailers with actionable analytics on customer
preferences.

2. Key Features & Product Capabilities
1. Real-Time 3D Video Fitting (Smart Mirror)
Live camera video feed overlays 3D/2D digital garments onto the customer's body with natural fabric draping,
scaling, and orientation as the customer moves.
2. Complete Brand Catalog Management (Recent & Past Collections) [Special Highlight]
Stores can upload and organize their complete apparel catalog—including current stock, seasonal launches,
and archived past collections. Customers can browse through all historical and present designs, pick any shirt,
jacket, or traditional wear, and immediately preview it on themselves. If an older item is selected, the system can
notify store staff or facilitate pre-orders.
3. Interactive UI & Customization
Touchscreen / Gesture-controlled interface allowing users to switch colors, try different sizes (S, M, L, XL),
combine tops and bottoms, and compare outfits side-by-side.
4. Automated Size & Fit Recommendation
Extracts shoulder-width, chest, waist, and height measurements directly from camera keypoints to recommend
the ideal garment size to the user.
